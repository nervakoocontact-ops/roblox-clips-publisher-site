How it works

  1. Sign in with TikTok

    You authenticate with your own TikTok account through TikTok's official login and authorization screen. You can see and approve the permissions the app requests.

  2. Choose your own videos

    You pick the gameplay videos you want to share from your own computer. The app never selects or uploads a video on its own.

  3. Upload as a TikTok draft

    When you start an upload, the selected video is sent to TikTok and arrives in your TikTok account as a draft. Nothing is posted publicly by the app.

  4. Review and publish in TikTok

    You open the draft in TikTok, edit the caption and settings, and decide whether to publish it. Publishing always happens in TikTok, by you.

Your data, in short

  • Local first. Sign-in tokens are intended to be stored locally on your own computer. Roblox Clips Publisher does not currently use a cloud user database.
  • You choose every upload. A video is sent to TikTok only when you select it and start the upload.
  • No selling or ad sharing. Your information is not sold and is not shared with advertisers.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no advertising tracking, and no cookies on this website.
