How it works

  1. Prepare video content locally

    You create and edit your videos on your own computer. Source media and finished files stay in your local folders.

  2. Review the final media

    You check each finished video before anything leaves your computer. The app never selects or uploads a video on its own.

  3. Authenticate your own account

    You sign in to your own platform account through that platform's official login and authorization screen, where you see and approve the permissions requested.

  4. Upload to supported platforms

    When you start an upload, the selected video is sent directly to the platform you chose. TikTok uploads arrive in your TikTok account as drafts.

  5. Complete publication on the platform

    Where the platform requires it, you finish review, captions, settings and publishing yourself, inside that platform. For TikTok, publishing always happens in TikTok, by you.

Workflows

Content Studio is designed to support creators who run more than one channel or content format. It can support workflows for:

  • Gaming short-form content prepared from your own recordings.
  • Movie recap content, in short-form and long-form formats.
  • Multi-channel preparation, keeping videos for different channels organized locally.
  • YouTube uploads to a channel you authorize.
  • TikTok draft uploads to your own TikTok inbox, using the video.upload permission. The app does not post to TikTok publicly.

Development is ongoing; features are added only as they are built and tested.

Your data, in short

  • Your own accounts. You authenticate each platform account yourself. Sign-in tokens are intended to remain on your own computer.
  • No cloud video storage. Content Studio does not operate its own cloud video-storage service or user database.
  • You choose every upload. A video is sent to a platform only when you select it and start the upload.
  • No selling or ad sharing. Your information is not sold and is not shared with advertisers.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no advertising tracking, and no cookies on this website.
