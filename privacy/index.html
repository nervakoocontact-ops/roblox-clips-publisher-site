Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026 · Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Content Studio (the "App"), a desktop application for creating, reviewing and uploading short-form and long-form video content across channels, handles your information. It also covers this website. The App is an independent project and is not affiliated with TikTok, YouTube, Google, or any other platform.

  • Stored on your computer. OAuth credentials, sign-in tokens and basic account details are intended to remain on your own computer. No cloud user database is currently used.
  • No cloud video storage. Content Studio does not operate its own cloud video-storage service.
  • You choose every upload. A video is sent to a platform only when you select it and start the upload.
  • Never sold. Your information is not sold and is not shared with advertisers.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no advertising tracking, and no cookies on this website.

1. Information we collect

When you connect the App to a platform account (for example TikTok or YouTube), the App may receive:

  • Basic account information made available by that platform, such as an app-specific account identifier (for TikTok, the open_id), avatar, display name, or channel name.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by the platform, which let the App act for you within the permissions you approved.
  • Upload results returned by the platform, such as an upload or publish identifier and processing status.

The App also works with the video files you choose on your computer. A video is sent to a platform only when you select it and start an upload. To avoid accidental duplicate uploads, the App may keep a local upload history containing file names, file sizes, content fingerprints (hashes), timestamps, and the identifiers and statuses returned by the platform.

We do not collect your platform passwords. You enter them only on each platform's own login screen. The App does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and this website sets no cookies.

2. How we use information

  • To sign you in to the platform accounts you choose to connect.
  • To identify which account is connected.
  • To upload the videos you select to those accounts, and to report upload and processing status back to you.
  • To help you avoid uploading the same file twice.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to these features.

3. Platform integrations

The App uses each platform's official OAuth authorization for sign-in. On the platform's consent screen you can see the permissions the App requests before you approve them.

TikTok. The App uses:

  • Basic user information, to identify the connected account (open_id, avatar, display name).
  • The video.upload permission, to send the videos you select to your TikTok account as drafts. Drafts appear in your TikTok account, where you review, edit, and choose whether to publish them. The App does not publish content to TikTok publicly for you.

YouTube. The App uses the permissions needed to upload the videos you select to a YouTube channel you authorize, with the details and visibility settings you choose.

Selected media is sent directly from your computer to the platform you requested. Once information reaches a platform, that platform processes it under its own terms and privacy policy, for example TikTok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, or YouTube's Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not control how platforms handle your data.

4. Local storage

OAuth credentials, access and refresh tokens, basic account information, and the local upload history described above are intended to be stored locally on the computer where you use the App. They may be removed by deleting the App's local application data, or through a sign-out/revoke function in a future version of the App. Content Studio does not currently use a cloud user database or its own servers to store your account information, tokens, or videos.

Content Studio does not operate its own cloud video-storage service. When you start an upload, the video you selected is sent directly to the platform you chose. Your local source media remains on your computer unless you remove it.

5. Sharing of information

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not share personal information with advertisers.
  • The videos you choose, and the tokens needed to authorize uploads, are sent to the platform you selected (for example TikTok or YouTube), because that is how the upload feature works.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, but the App does not hold user data on our own servers.

6. Data retention

  • Tokens, basic account information and local upload history are intended to be stored on your computer until you delete the App's local application data, or, for tokens, until they expire or are revoked.
  • Video files: Content Studio does not upload your videos to its own cloud servers. The video you select is sent directly to the platform when you start an upload, and your local source media remains on your computer unless you remove it.
  • Uploaded drafts and published videos are kept by each platform under that platform's policies. You can delete them on the platform.

7. Security

Sign-in and uploads are intended to take place directly between your computer and each platform's services, which use encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Because credentials and tokens are intended to be stored locally, the security of your computer and your operating-system user account matters. Use a password or other device protection, and do not share your computer account with people you don't trust. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we aim to handle your information carefully.

8. User choices

  • Choose what to upload: nothing is uploaded unless you select a video and start the upload.
  • Review before publishing: every TikTok upload arrives as a draft that you control in TikTok.
  • Remove stored tokens: you can remove locally stored tokens by deleting the App's local application data. A sign-out/revoke function may be provided in a future version.
  • Revoke access: you can remove the App's access at any time in your TikTok account settings or your Google account permissions.
  • Delete local data: deleting the App's local application data removes the stored tokens, account details and upload history from your computer.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age required to use the connected platforms in your country. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the App, please contact us. Deleting the App's local application data removes the locally stored tokens and account details from that computer.

10. Changes to this policy

If the App's data practices change, for example if a cloud service or a new platform integration is added, we will update this policy before the change takes effect and change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be pointed out on the home page of this website.
