Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026 · Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Roblox Clips Publisher (the "App"), a desktop application for uploading short-form gameplay videos to TikTok as drafts, handles your information. It also covers this website. The App is an independent project and is not affiliated with TikTok or Roblox.

  • Stored on your computer. Sign-in tokens and basic account details are intended to be stored locally on your own computer. No cloud user database is currently used.
  • You choose every upload. A video is sent to TikTok only when you select it and start the upload.
  • Never sold. Your information is not sold and is not shared with advertisers.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no advertising tracking, and no cookies on this website.

1. Information we collect

When you connect the App to TikTok, the App may receive:

  • Basic TikTok account information, such as your open_id (an app-specific identifier), avatar, and display name.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, which let the App act for you within the permissions you approved.

The App also works with the video files you choose on your computer. A video is sent to TikTok only when you select it and start an upload.

We do not collect your TikTok password. You enter it only on TikTok's own login screen. The App does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and this website sets no cookies.

2. How we use information

  • To sign you in with TikTok.
  • To identify which TikTok account is connected (for example, by its display name and avatar).
  • To upload the videos you select to your TikTok account as drafts.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to these features.

3. TikTok integration

The App uses TikTok's official OAuth authorization for sign-in. On TikTok's consent screen you can see the permissions the App requests before you approve them. The App uses:

  • Basic user information, to identify the connected account (open_id, avatar, display name).
  • The video.upload permission, to send the videos you select to your TikTok account as drafts.

Videos are transmitted to TikTok only when you start an upload. Uploaded drafts appear in your TikTok account, where you review, edit, and choose whether to publish them. The App does not publish content publicly for you.

Once information reaches TikTok, TikTok processes it under its own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. We do not control how TikTok handles your data.

4. Local storage

OAuth access and refresh tokens, and the basic account information described above, are intended to be stored locally on the computer where you use the App. They may be removed by deleting the App's local application data, or through a sign-out/revoke function in a future version of the App. Roblox Clips Publisher does not currently use a cloud user database or its own servers to store your account information, tokens, or videos.

Roblox Clips Publisher does not upload your videos to its own cloud servers. When you start an upload, the video you selected is sent directly to TikTok. Your local source files may remain on your own computer.

5. Sharing of information

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not share personal information with advertisers.
  • The videos you choose, and the tokens needed to authorize uploads, are sent to TikTok, because that is how the upload feature works.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, but the App does not hold user data on our own servers.

6. Data retention

  • Tokens and basic account information are intended to be stored on your computer until you delete the App's local application data, or the tokens expire or are revoked.
  • Video files: Roblox Clips Publisher does not upload your videos to its own cloud servers. The video you select is sent directly to TikTok when you start an upload, and your local source files may remain on your own computer.
  • Uploaded drafts and published videos are kept by TikTok under TikTok's policies. You can delete them in TikTok.

7. Security

Sign-in and uploads are intended to take place directly between your computer and TikTok's services, which use encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Because tokens are intended to be stored locally, the security of your computer and your operating-system user account matters. Use a password or other device protection, and do not share your computer account with people you don't trust. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we aim to handle your information carefully.

8. User choices

  • Choose what to upload: nothing is uploaded unless you select a video and start the upload.
  • Review before publishing: every upload arrives as a draft that you control in TikTok.
  • Remove stored tokens: you can remove locally stored tokens by deleting the App's local application data. A sign-out/revoke function may be provided in a future version.
  • Revoke access: you can remove the App's access at any time in your TikTok account settings.
  • Delete local data: deleting the App's local application data removes the stored tokens and account details from your computer.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age required to use TikTok in your country. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the App, please contact us. Deleting the App's local application data removes the locally stored tokens and account details from that computer.

10. Changes to this policy

If the App's data practices change, for example if a cloud service is ever added, we will update this policy before the change takes effect and change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be pointed out on the home page of this website.
